# Boss stages and practice

The stage menu groups encounters by mechanics and applies these multipliers
on top of the existing time and co-op boss scaling. Movement speed is unchanged.

| Tier | HP | Damage | Encounters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 0.8 | 0.85 | Darmanitan, Wishiwashi, Aegislash, Lake trio, Birds |
| Medium | 1 | 1 | Zygarde, Rotom, Meloetta, Hoopa, Beasts, Swords, Eon, Moon, Tower |
| Hard | 1.25 | 1.15 | Necrozma, Deoxys, Terapagos, Weather, Creation, Tao, Tapu, Ruin, Regi, Heroes |

Easy encounters have more limited effects or attack patterns. Medium encounters
add changing forms, movement or team support. Hard encounters combine stronger
defenses, multiple threats, restrictive terrain or layered attacks. Ruin remains
locked until its missing sprites are installed. Hurry draws exactly one playable
stage per tier and plays Easy → Medium → Hard. The draws are uniform within
each tier and are shared by the host, so all co-op players fight the same stages.

These tiers are provisional design judgments, not measured win rates. Their
HP/damage multipliers are balance settings applied after classification, not
evidence for the classification itself. For example, Birds and Beasts both use
dash trails; Eon's healing and enrage can be harder than its Medium label;
Darmanitan's Zen damage reduction can prolong an Easy fight; and piercing
attacks can make Heroes easier by bypassing the shield. Player build, targeting
order, party size and terrain matter. Re-tiering should use comparable builds
and observed clear times, damage taken and failures.

Palkia no longer varies movement speed or curves projectiles. While alive, it
teleports every living player every eight seconds, with a one-second warning.
The host chooses random positions inside the arena, away from walls, bosses,
other landing positions and active damaging fields. If no valid spot is found,
that player stays in place. Players receive 0.75 seconds of invulnerability on
arrival. Defeating Palkia cancels pending teleports; specials pause the timer.

Arena radii grow by 20% of the solo radius per additional player, rounded up.
Boss/Rift radii for 1–10 players are 11, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 25, 27, 29 and 31 tiles. Standard's Arceus
arena uses the same rule with a solo radius of 24. Party size is captured on
arena entry and the host sends the radius to clients. The overworld is unbounded.

In **Chọn màn đấu**, use **Thử sân co-op** to simulate 1–10 players' arena size
and boss HP in solo practice, then pick a stage from any tier. This creates no
fake teammates or network connections. Optionally enable **Bật bất tử khi vào
màn**, and use the existing in-game toggle to turn it off. Choose **Bộ ba Sáng
thế** to observe Palkia's natural teleport timing; there is no force-teleport
button. Practice does not save run rewards.
